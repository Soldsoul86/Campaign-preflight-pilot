Privacy and limitations

What this pilot does and does not do

Campaign checks run in your own browser. The current application does not call an AI service, upload your pasted content, send your content to an analytics provider, or offer payment collection.

Your content

The tool processes fields locally in JavaScript; reports are generated as files on your device only when you select Download. No account or login is requested. A hosting service may receive normal requests and retain technical access logs when you open this webpage.

Do not paste confidential, regulated or personal data into an experimental tool. Remove customer information first.

Scope

This is a deterministic text comparison, not semantic AI verification. It does not visit URLs, check live redirects, exercise a coupon, confirm a price at checkout, check recipient segments, or test mail delivery, authentication, accessibility, or legal compliance. It can produce false positives and miss serious errors.

Commercial status

This pilot is free. No paid product is yet available, and no billing or subscription is enabled. This website is not affiliated with Litmus, Klaviyo, Synapse Retention or any source cited in our evidence pages.
